Small, supportive steps

What might help next

Choose one gentle idea to try today. These suggestions complement professional care; they never replace it.

01

Make the day smaller

Pick one essential task and one kind thing for yourself. A short walk, shower, or meal counts.

02

Protect your rest

Keep a consistent wind-down time, dim screens before bed, and speak with a professional if sleep stays difficult.

03

Stay connected

Tell one trusted person, “I have had a hard couple of weeks and could use some company.”

04

Give your mind a pause

Try five slow breaths, a brief journal entry, or a study/work break with no performance goal.

05

Find qualified support

A psychologist, counsellor, or doctor can help you understand what is happening and discuss options.
