The project

Built to make the first step feel possible.

AI-Based Depression Detection and Support System is a college project from Allenhouse Institute of Technology.

Problem statement

People may notice changes in mood, sleep, energy, or concentration but not know how to interpret them or where to begin seeking help.

Proposed solution

A private web experience that combines a validated screening questionnaire with careful language, progress context, and routes toward human support.
